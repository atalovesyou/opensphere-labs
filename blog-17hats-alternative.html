What 17hats Does Well (Honest Take)

We're not going to pretend 17hats is bad — it's a solid product for freelancers who want one app for everything. Here's where it genuinely wins:

  • All-in-one convenience: CRM, proposals, contracts, invoicing, bookkeeping, and scheduling in one place. No tool-hopping.
  • Built-in bookkeeping: Real expense tracking and profit/loss — our stack needs Wave for this.
  • Built-in scheduling: Calendar booking integrated with CRM — our stack needs Calendly.
  • CRM with history: Full client database with email history, project notes, and timelines — our tools use browser localStorage.
  • Established platform: Since 2013, reliable, well-supported, with a community.

The question is: are those conveniences worth $720/year? If you're a solo freelancer doing under $100K, probably not. If you're a multi-person shop with complex workflows, maybe.

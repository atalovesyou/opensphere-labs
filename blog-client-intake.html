What Is AI Client Intake?

AI client intake replaces your static "Contact Us" form with a conversational chatbot that:

  • Asks adaptive questions — the next question changes based on the previous answer
  • Qualifies leads in real-time — scores them Hot 🔥 / Warm / Cool / Cold
  • Extracts structured data — name, budget, timeline, project type, industry
  • Works 24/7 — qualifies leads while you sleep
  • Hands you hot leads — you only spend time on qualified prospects

Think of it as a virtual assistant that never sleeps and never forgets to ask about budget.

Why Static Forms Are Killing Your Conversion Rate

Traditional intake forms have three fatal flaws:

Problem #1

They ask the same questions to everyone. A startup founder needs different qualification than a law firm partner. Static forms can't adapt.

Problem #2

They don't score leads. You get 10 form submissions and have no idea which ones are worth your time. You end up responding to all of them equally.

Problem #3

They have terrible completion rates. Long forms with 15 fields? Most people bounce after field 6. Conversational bots maintain engagement through each question.

The result: you waste hours on unqualified leads while hot prospects slip through the cracks.

The 5-Step AI Intake Process

Step 1 — Greeting & Type Detection

The bot asks: "What brings you here today?" Based on the response, it identifies the project type (web design, legal consultation, coaching, etc.) and selects the right conversation template.

Step 2 — Adaptive Qualification

Instead of a fixed form, the bot asks context-aware follow-ups. If someone says "I need a website," it asks about timeline, budget range, and current site status. If someone says "I need legal help," it asks about practice area, jurisdiction, and urgency.

Step 3 — Budget Screening

The bot naturally surfaces budget expectations without being awkward: "To give you the best recommendation, what range are you working with?" This eliminates the 80% of leads who can't afford you — before you spend a minute on them.

Step 4 — Lead Scoring

AI scores the lead based on: budget fit, urgency signals, project clarity, and contact completeness. You get a simple Hot 🔥 / Warm / Cool / Cold rating with the reasoning.

Step 5 — Structured Handoff

The bot outputs a clean JSON object with all extracted data — ready for your CRM, email, or project management tool. No manual data entry.

Your Intake Checklist: What to Capture

Every intake conversation should extract these essentials:

  • Contact info — Name, email, phone (at minimum)
  • Project type — What they need (auto-detected from their words)
  • Budget range — Can they afford your minimum?
  • Timeline — When do they need it? Urgency = hot lead.
  • Current situation — What are they using now? Pain points?
  • Decision authority — Are they the decision-maker or need approval?
  • Previous experience — Have they hired for this before?
  • Qualification score — Auto-calculated fit rating

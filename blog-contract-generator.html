8 Clauses Every Freelance Contract Needs (And What Happens Without Them)

Clause What It Does Risk Without It
Scope of Work Defines exactly what you'll deliver Unlimited scope creep, free extra work
Payment Terms Amount, schedule, method, late fees Late or partial payments, no recourse
Intellectual Property Who owns the work and when ownership transfers Client claims your portfolio pieces, process, or tools
Revision Policy How many revisions are included, cost of extras Endless revision loops with no extra pay
Kill Fee Payment if client cancels mid-project Zero compensation for completed work
Termination How either party can end the agreement Stuck in a bad project with no exit
NDA / Confidentiality Protects sensitive information both ways Client shares your process, or you leak theirs
Late Payment Penalty Interest or fee on overdue invoices Client sits on invoices for months

FAQ

Is an AI-generated contract legally binding?

Yes. A contract is legally binding if it includes an offer, acceptance, consideration (payment), and mutual intent — regardless of who wrote it. AI-generated contracts meet these requirements. For high-value projects ($10K+), have a lawyer review the final draft.

What clauses should every freelance contract include?

Scope of work, payment terms, timeline, revision policy, IP ownership, termination clause, and confidentiality/NDA if relevant. ContractPilot includes all of these by default — just toggle the ones you need.

How much does a lawyer charge for a freelance contract?

$500–$2,000 depending on complexity. AI generators produce equivalent contracts for $0–$9/month. For most projects under $10K, AI is sufficient. Over $10K, get a lawyer to review the AI draft.

What's the difference between contract generation and contract review?

Generation creates a new contract from your project description — you send it to the client. Review analyzes a contract the client sent you and flags risky clauses. ContractPilot does both.

Can I use a free template instead?

Templates work for simple projects but risk being generic, outdated, or not valid in your jurisdiction. AI generators customize each contract to your specific project, client, and local requirements.

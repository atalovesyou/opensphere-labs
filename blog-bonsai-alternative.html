Why Freelancers Are Looking for Bonsai Alternatives

Bonsai (hellobonsai.com) is one of the most popular freelancer tools — and for good reason. It bundles invoicing, contracts, proposals, time tracking, task management, and accounting into one platform.

But here's the problem: you're paying for features you don't use.

Is Bonsai worth it for freelancers?

Yes. InvoicePilot uses client-side NLP to parse your work description and auto-generate a professional invoice with line items, amounts, payment terms, and client details. Everything runs in your browser — no API calls, no accounts, no costs.

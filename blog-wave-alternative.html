When Wave Still Makes Sense

Fair comparison matters. Wave is still the right choice if you need:

  • Full double-entry accounting — Income statements, balance sheets, cash flow
  • Bank reconciliation — Connect bank accounts, auto-categorize transactions
  • Receipt scanning — Snap photos, auto-extract amounts
  • US/Canada payroll — Tax calculations, direct deposit, filings
  • Payment processing — Accept credit cards / ACH directly through invoices

If you're a solo freelancer who just needs to send invoices and get paid, Wave is overkill — and the ads are annoying. InvoicePilot does the one thing you need, faster and cleaner.

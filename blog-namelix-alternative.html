NameForge's 16 Naming Strategies (Why It Generates Better Names)

Most name generators use 1-2 strategies. NameForge uses 16, which means more creative variety and less chance of getting the same name as everyone else:

  1. Compound — Combine two words (Facebook, WordPress)
  2. Portmanteau — Blend two words (Instagram, Pinterest)
  3. Abstract — Invented words that sound right (Kodak, Rolex)
  4. Metaphor — Symbolic names that tell a story (Amazon, Salesforce)
  5. Action Verb — Do-something names (Zoom, Slack, Stripe)
  6. Domain-Style — Web-friendly names (about.me, not.ai)
  7. Acronym — Shortened or initials-based (IBM, KFC)
  8. Founder Name — Personal brand (Hewlett-Packard, Ben & Jerry's)
  9. Foreign Word — Non-English with meaning (Volvo, Häagen-Dazs)
  10. Misspelling — Intentional typo (Lyft, Fiverr)
  11. Number + Word — Numeric combinations (7-Eleven, 3M)
  12. Prefix/Suffix — Modified roots (Spotify, Shopify)
  13. Descriptive — Says what it does (General Motors, American Airlines)
  14. Alliteration — Repeated sounds (PayPal, Dunkin' Donuts)
  15. Contrast — Opposites or tension (Tinder, Yelp)
  16. Evocative — Emotional resonance without literal meaning (Virgin, Apple)

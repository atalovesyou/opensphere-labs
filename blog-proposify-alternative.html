Why Freelancers Are Looking for Proposify Alternatives

Proposify is a solid proposal tool — for agencies with budgets. At $49/mo per user (Essentials) or $79/mo (Advanced), it's a serious commitment for freelancers and solopreneurs who send 5–15 proposals a month.

Here's what you're actually paying for in Proposify:

  • Team approval workflows (you don't need these solo)
  • Sales analytics dashboards (nice, not essential)
  • CRM integrations like Salesforce, HubSpot (overkill for freelancers)
  • Content library with pre-approved blocks (useful but not $588/yr useful)

Most freelancers need exactly 4 things from a proposal tool: create proposals fast, make them look professional, track whether the client opened them, and export to PDF. You shouldn't pay $49/mo for that.

FAQ

Is there a completely free alternative to Proposify?

Yes. ProposalPilot is completely free with unlimited proposals, AI generation from natural language, 3 templates, NLP scope parsing, pricing configuration, PDF export, and proposal history. No account required.

Why is Proposify so expensive?

Proposify is priced for agencies and sales teams ($49–$79/user/month). You're paying for team features: approval workflows, role permissions, CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot), content libraries, and advanced analytics. Solo freelancers rarely need these features.

Can I really generate a proposal with AI for free?

Yes. ProposalPilot uses client-side NLP to parse your project description and auto-generate a structured proposal with scope breakdown, timeline, pricing table, and terms. Everything runs in your browser — no API calls, no accounts, no costs.

What about e-signatures?

ProposalPilot doesn't include e-signatures. If you need legally binding signatures, PandaDoc ($35/mo) or Bonsai ($17/mo) are better options. For most freelancers, a PDF proposal sent via email with a simple "reply to confirm" workflow works fine.

Is ProposalPilot safe for confidential proposals?

Yes. ProposalPilot runs entirely in your browser using localStorage. Your proposals are never uploaded to any server. When you close the tab, your data stays on your device only.

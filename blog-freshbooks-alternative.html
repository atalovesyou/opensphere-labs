Why Freelancers Are Looking Beyond FreshBooks

FreshBooks is the biggest name in freelancer invoicing — but the pricing tells a different story:

  • Lite: $19/mo — capped at 5 billable clients (that's $3.80/client/month)
  • Plus: $33/mo — unlimited clients, but you're paying for accounting features you may never use
  • Premium: $60/mo — adds advanced reports, custom emails, team members

At $228-$720/year, you're paying for double-entry accounting, expense tracking, project management, and mileage logging. Sound familiar? Most freelancers use maybe 30% of what FreshBooks offers. The other 70% is bloat you're subsidizing.

Here's the truth: you don't need a full accounting suite to send invoices. You need to describe your work, get a professional invoice, send it, and know when it's paid.

2. FreshBooks — The Incumbent

FreshBooks is the default choice — solid accounting, but overkill for most freelancers who just need to send invoices.

✅ Pros

  • Full double-entry accounting
  • Expense tracking with receipt scanning
  • Time tracking built in
  • Integrates with every payment processor
  • Strong mobile app
  • Mileage tracking

❌ Cons

  • $19/mo minimum, $228/year
  • Lite plan caps at 5 billable clients
  • No AI invoice generation
  • Feature bloat — most freelancers use 30%
  • Price increases after first year discounts
  • Overkill if you just need to send invoices

3. Wave — Best Free Accounting

Wave gives you actual accounting for free — but the invoicing is basic and there's no AI.

✅ Pros

  • Completely free accounting & invoicing
  • Bank connection & reconciliation
  • Receipt scanning
  • Payment processing (2.9% + 60¢/card)
  • Reports: P&L, balance sheet, cash flow

❌ Cons

  • No AI invoice generation
  • Limited templates (basic customization)
  • No auto-chase or follow-up emails
  • Must create an account
  • Clunky UI — feels like a 2015 product
  • Ads for their paid bookkeeping service

4. Bonsai — All-in-One Freelancer Suite

Bonsai (HelloBonsai) bundles invoicing with contracts, proposals, and time tracking. Solid product, but the price adds up.

✅ Pros

  • Contracts + proposals + invoicing in one
  • Automated payment reminders
  • 5+ invoice templates
  • Expense tracking
  • Scheduling and CRM

❌ Cons

  • $24-39/mo ($288-468/year)
  • Limited AI — basic automation, not generative
  • No free tier (14-day trial only)
  • Feature overlap with FreshBooks
  • Workflow builder feels rigid

5. Invoice Ninja — Best Open Source

Invoice Ninja is powerful and open source. Self-host for free or pay for the cloud version.

✅ Pros

  • Open source — self-host free forever
  • 10+ invoice templates
  • Recurring invoices & auto-billing
  • Client portal
  • Payment integration (Stripe, PayPal, etc.)

❌ Cons

  • Self-hosting requires technical setup
  • Cloud version is $14/mo
  • UI is functional but not beautiful
  • No AI invoice generation
  • Learning curve for non-technical users

6. Zoho Invoice — Best for Volume

Zoho Invoice is free for up to 1,000 invoices/year. Good if you need lots of invoices but don't need AI.

✅ Pros

  • Free for up to 1,000 invoices/year
  • 15+ professional templates
  • Automated payment reminders
  • Client portal with approval workflows
  • Multi-currency & multi-language

❌ Cons

  • Zoho account required
  • No AI invoice generation
  • Part of the Zoho ecosystem (upsell pressure)
  • Can feel heavy for simple invoicing
  • 1,000 invoice limit on free tier

7. Hiveage

Hiveage focuses on billing and invoicing for small teams. Clean but not free.

✅ Pros

  • Estimates + invoices + recurring billing
  • Time & expense tracking
  • Multi-currency
  • Payment gateway integration
  • Simple, clean interface

❌ Cons

  • $15/mo ($180/year)
  • No free tier
  • No AI features
  • Limited integrations vs competitors
  • Smaller community & support

8. And.co (by Fiverr)

Fiverr's freelancer tool — contracts, invoices, and scheduling bundled together.

✅ Pros

  • Contracts + invoices + scheduling
  • 3 invoice templates
  • Payment reminders
  • Integrated with Fiverr ecosystem

❌ Cons

  • $18/mo ($216/year)
  • Free tier extremely limited
  • No AI invoice generation
  • Tied to Fiverr platform
  • Fewer integrations than competitors

Frequently Asked Questions

What is scope creep?

Scope creep is when a client requests work outside the original project agreement without additional compensation. Common examples: "Can you also add a contact form?", "I know we said 3 pages, but can we make it 5?", "This should only take you 10 minutes, right?"

Can AI really detect scope creep?

Yes. By comparing client requests against your original contract or SOW, AI can flag language that indicates out-of-scope work. It's not perfect (human judgment still matters), but it catches patterns humans miss, especially in long email threads.

Do I need scope management if I have a good contract?

Yes. A good contract defines scope, but it doesn't detect when scope is being violated. Tools like ScopeGuard bridge that gap — your contract defines what's in scope, and the AI watches for violations in real-time.

What's the difference between scope creep and feature creep?

Scope creep = client asks for more than agreed. Feature creep = you (or your team) keep adding features nobody asked for. Both cost money. Scope creep tools focus on the client side; project management discipline handles the internal side.

FAQ

What is the best free scope creep detector?

ScopeGuard by OpenSphere Labs is the best free option — it offers unlimited scope creep analysis, contract parsing, client tracking, and PDF reports at no cost. Most competitors charge $15-30/mo for similar features.

How do AI scope creep detectors work?

AI scope creep detectors analyze your contract or statement of work, then compare incoming client requests against the agreed scope. They flag out-of-scope requests, calculate a creep score, and generate professional pushback scripts you can send to clients.

How much does scope creep cost freelancers?

The average freelancer loses $6,000–15,600 per year to scope creep, according to industry surveys. 71% of freelancers report experiencing scope creep regularly, with 34% seeing project budgets eaten by 25% or more.

Can scope creep detectors integrate with project management tools?

Most scope creep detectors are standalone tools in 2026. Some, like ScopeCatch, offer Slack integration. Full project management integration (Asana, Monday, etc.) is still emerging in this product category.

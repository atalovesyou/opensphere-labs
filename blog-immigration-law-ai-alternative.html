FAQ

Is ImmigrationAgent.ai really free?

Yes. All core features — eligibility checks, document checklists, form guidance, and case assessment — are completely free with no signup, no login, and no credit card required. Use it as many times as you need.

Can AI replace an immigration lawyer?

No. AI tools provide guidance, eligibility analysis, and document preparation help — but they cannot provide legal advice or represent you in court. For complex cases (deportation defense, appeals, complicated eligibility questions), always consult a licensed immigration attorney. AI tools are best used to understand your situation before seeking professional help.

What visa types does ImmigrationAgent.ai cover?

ImmigrationAgent.ai covers 20+ visa categories including: H-1B, O-1, L-1, TN, E-2, EB-1, EB-2, EB-3, EB-5, F-1, J-1, B-1/B-2, green cards (family, employment, diversity lottery), citizenship/naturalization, DACA, asylum, TPS, and more.

How accurate is AI immigration guidance?

AI tools provide helpful guidance based on publicly available immigration law and policy, but accuracy varies with case complexity. Always verify requirements on official government websites (USCIS.gov for US, IRCC.gc.ca for Canada). Use AI as a starting point, not a final answer.

What's the cheapest way to get immigration help?

Start with free AI tools like ImmigrationAgent.ai to understand your eligibility and requirements. Then use official government resources (USCIS, IRCC) to file directly. If your case is complex, look for non-profit legal aid organizations that offer free or low-cost immigration assistance before paying $3,000-10,000+ for private attorneys.

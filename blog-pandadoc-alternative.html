When PandaDoc IS Worth Paying For

Let's be fair. PandaDoc is genuinely better for some use cases:

  • Enterprise teams (10+ people) — approval workflows, role-based access, SSO
  • High-volume sales orgs — CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot native), automated document generation
  • Legal compliance requirements — SOC 2, HIPAA, advanced audit trails
  • API-heavy workflows — PandaDoc's API is mature and well-documented

If you're a solo freelancer or small agency sending 5-20 proposals/month, none of these apply. You're paying for features you'll never use.

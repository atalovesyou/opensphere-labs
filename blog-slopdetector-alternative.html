5 Things AI Slop Tools Should Catch

  1. Filler phrases — "In today's fast-paced world", "It's important to note that", "At the end of the day"
  2. Hedging language — "It could be argued that", "Some might say", excessive use of "potentially", "possibly", "arguably"
  3. Buzzword overload — "leverage synergies", "disrupt the paradigm", "innovative solutions" without specificity
  4. Vague assertions — "Studies show", "Experts agree", "Research indicates" with no citations
  5. Repetitive structure — Same sentence pattern used 3+ times in a row, formulaic paragraph openings

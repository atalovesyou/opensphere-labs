FAQ

Is Clockify really free?

Yes, for core features: unlimited users, unlimited projects, billable rates, basic reports, and time tracking across web, mobile, and desktop. Premium features (audit log, custom fields, required fields) are paid, but most freelancers never need them.

Can I import my Toggl data into Clockify?

Yes. Clockify has a built-in Toggl import tool. Export your data from Toggl (Settings → Export), then import into Clockify (Projects → Import). Your time entries, projects, and clients transfer over.

Does InvoicePilot connect to Clockify?

Not directly — but the workflow is simple: export your hours from Clockify, paste them into InvoicePilot as natural language ("8 hours design at $90/hr"), and get a formatted invoice in seconds. Faster than any API integration for most freelancers.

What if I need accounting, not just invoicing?

Pair InvoicePilot with Wave Accounting (free, but ad-supported) or GnuCash (free, open source, no ads) for full double-entry bookkeeping. InvoicePilot handles the quick invoice generation; the accounting tool handles the books.

Is my data safe with these free tools?

InvoicePilot is 100% client-side — your data never leaves your browser. Clockify stores data on their servers but offers data export and deletion at any time. Both are more private than most paid alternatives.

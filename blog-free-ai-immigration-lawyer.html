Immigration AI + Tax AI: The Complete Immigrant Toolkit

Immigration and taxes are deeply connected. Moving countries triggers tax obligations most immigrants don't know about:

  • FBAR — $10,000+ in foreign accounts? You must report it (penalty: up to $100K for willful failure)

  • Treaty benefits — The US has tax treaties with 60+ countries that can reduce withholding taxes
  • Exit taxes — Giving up a green card? You might owe taxes on unrealized gains

When You Still Need a Real Immigration Lawyer

AI is powerful, but it's not magic. Get a human attorney if:

  • You have a criminal record (even minor offenses can affect immigration)
  • You've received a denial, RFE, or NOID
  • You're in removal/deportation proceedings
  • You need a waiver (I-601, I-212, etc.)
  • Your case involves national security or espionage questions
  • You're applying for asylum (these cases are complex and fact-specific)

Think of AI immigration tools like WebMD for immigration. Great for understanding your situation, knowing what to ask, and preparing for your attorney appointment. Not a substitute for the appointment itself.

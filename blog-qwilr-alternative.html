Why Look for a Qwilr Alternative?

Qwilr is known for turning proposals into beautiful, interactive web pages with analytics, e-signatures, and CRM integrations. It's impressive — but it has real drawbacks for freelancers:

  • $35–$59/month — expensive for solo freelancers who send 2–5 proposals/month

  • No AI proposal generation — you still write everything from scratch
  • No pricing intelligence — no market data or AI pricing suggestions
  • Overkill for most freelance work — built for agencies, not solopreneurs
  • Analytics lock-in — proposal tracking only works within their ecosystem

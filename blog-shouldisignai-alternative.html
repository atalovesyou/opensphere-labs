The 5 Red Flags AI Tools Catch (That Humans Miss)

1. Unlimited Revision Clauses
"Client may request reasonable revisions" = unlimited free work. AI flags this instantly. Fix: Cap at 2-3 revision rounds.
2. IP Assignment Before Payment
"All work product is owned by Client upon creation" = they own it before you're paid. Fix: IP transfers only upon full payment.
3. Non-Compete Hidden in NDAs
Broad non-competes prevent you from working in your industry. AI catches these buried in Section 14 of a 20-page contract. Fix: Restrict to direct competitors, 6 months max.
4. Vague Scope Definitions
"And other tasks as needed" is scope creep in writing. AI flags undefined deliverables. Fix: Attach a specific SoW with numbered deliverables.
5. Indemnification Traps
"Contractor shall indemnify Client against all claims" = you're on the hook for everything. Fix: Mutual indemnification, capped at project value.

No. AI contract review tools flag common red flags, unusual clauses, and potential risks — but they're not legal advice. Use them as a first-pass filter to identify issues, then consult a lawyer for high-stakes contracts ($10K+). This hybrid approach saves 80% on legal costs.

What contract red flags should freelancers watch for?

The top 5: unlimited revision clauses, IP assignment before payment, hidden non-compete clauses in NDAs, vague scope definitions ("and other tasks as needed"), and one-sided indemnification. All AI tools above catch these, but ContractPilot also gives you pushback scripts for each.

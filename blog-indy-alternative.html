Why Look for an Indy Alternative?

Indy (formerly HelloIndy) is a popular freelancer platform that bundles contracts, proposals, invoices, time tracking, task management, and file storage into one tool. It's decent if you want everything in one place — but it has real drawbacks:

  • $9–$20/month for features you may not use — paying for time tracking, task management, and file storage when you just need contracts
  • No AI contract review — generates contracts but won't flag risks in client agreements
  • No negotiation support — no scripts or pushback templates for unfair terms
  • Limited contract customization — basic templates without smart clause toggles
  • Vendor lock-in — all your contracts live in their platform

Is Indy worth $9/month for freelancers?

Indy is worth it if you use the full suite (contracts + proposals + invoicing + time tracking + file storage). At $9–$20/mo, it's a fair price for an all-in-one. But if you mainly need contracts, you're overpaying for features you don't use. ContractPilot handles contract generation and review — specifically and better — for $0.

Can I really generate contracts with AI for free?

Yes. ContractPilot uses AI-powered templates to generate complete contracts from your project description.

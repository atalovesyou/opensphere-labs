Why Switch from Typeform?

Typeform is excellent — nobody disputes that. But the pricing model creates problems for solo operators:

  • Free plan gutted: 10 responses/mo with Typeform branding — barely enough for testing
  • $25/mo for basics: The "Basic" plan still limits logic jumps and removes only some branding
  • $83/mo for business: Custom branding, CRM integrations, and removal of Typeform footer require the Business plan
  • Response limits on every tier: 10 (free), 100 (Basic), 1,000 (Plus), 10,000 (Business) — overages cost extra
  • No AI: Typeform is a static form builder. It doesn't adapt questions based on answers, score leads, or qualify prospects automatically

For most freelancers, the real question isn't "which form tool?" — it's "how do I qualify leads and intake clients without burning $300-1,000/year on a form builder?"

Every IntakeBot conversation produces a lead score from 0-100 based on budget, timeline, project fit, and responsiveness signals. Typeform gives you raw responses — you still need to manually triage every lead. IntakeBot sorts them automatically.

When to Stick with Typeform

Being honest — Typeform is still the right choice if you:

  • Need pixel-perfect branding control — Typeform's design customization is unmatched
  • Run surveys at scale (10,000+ responses) — Typeform's analytics are superior for research
  • Have deep integrations — Typeform connects to 200+ apps natively (HubSpot, Salesforce, Mailchimp, etc.)
  • Need payment collection in forms — Typeform integrates Stripe/Square natively

But if you're a freelancer or small business using Typeform for client intake and lead qualification, you're paying for features you don't need while missing the AI capabilities you do.

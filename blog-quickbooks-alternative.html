Why Freelancers Are Leaving QuickBooks in 2026

QuickBooks is built for small businesses with employees, inventory, and accountants. Here's what freelancers actually experience:

  • The $360-2,400/year tax — Simple Start is $30/mo. If you need multi-currency or more users, you're at $60-200/mo.
  • Accountant-centric UI — double-entry accounting, chart of accounts, reconciliation. Freelancers just want to send invoices and get paid.
  • Aggressive upselling — payroll, payments, time tracking, mileage — each is an extra cost.
  • Steep learning curve — most freelancers spend 2-3 hours just setting up QuickBooks. An invoice tool should take 0 minutes.

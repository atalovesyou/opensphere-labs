5 Scope Creep Red Flags AI Catches Instantly

1. "While you're at it..." requests

The classic creep entry point. Client asks for "one small thing" that wasn't in the SOW. AI flags it as a feature expansion — outside original deliverables.

2. "Can we make it more modern?"

Subjective design feedback that opens an infinite loop. AI flags design scope expansion — original SOW had 2 revision rounds, this is #4.

3. "We need this by Friday instead of Monday"

Timeline compression without adjusted payment. AI flags timeline creep — original deadline was agreed in contract.

4. "The budget is tight, but there's more work later"

Promise of future work to justify underpaying now. AI flags budget creep — work should be priced at market rate regardless.

5. "Can you also handle [entirely different service]?"

Client expands deliverables into areas you don't even offer. AI flags deliverable expansion — clearly outside stated project scope.

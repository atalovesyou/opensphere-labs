Why Calendly Falls Short for Freelancers

Calendly is a scheduling tool, not a client management tool. Here's what you're missing:

1. No Lead Qualification

Anyone with your link can book. No budget check. No project scope. No "is this even a fit?" filter. You end up on 30-minute calls with people who can't afford you.

2. No Intake Data

Calendly collects name, email, and maybe one custom question. You show up to calls with zero context. First 15 minutes = discovery that should have happened asynchronously.

3. The Free Tier is Aggressively Limited

One event type. No team scheduling. No reminders. No routing. To get anything useful, you're on Standard at $12/mo minimum.

What Calendly Does Well (Honest Take)

Fair's fair. Calendly excels at:

  • Calendar integration — real-time availability sync across Google, Outlook, iCloud
  • Automated reminders — SMS + email reduce no-shows by ~30%
  • Team routing — round-robin, weighted distribution for sales teams
  • Payment integration — charge at booking time via Stripe
  • Workflow automation — Zapier/Make integrations out of the box

If you're a sales team with 5+ reps doing high-volume round-robin booking, Calendly Teams is worth the $16/seat. For solo freelancers and small agencies? You're overpaying for a calendar widget.
